Break Escape System

Break Escape is a defensive mechanic that allows players to escape enemy combos by spending a limited combat resource.

To activate Break Escape, the player must first be hit by an enemy attack. The system cannot be activated freely; the enemy player must successfully land a hit and begin a combo before Break Escape becomes available.

Once the player is being hit:

Hold LMB + RMB together to charge Break Escape.
Continue holding for several seconds until the desired Break level is reached.
When the required resource cost is reached, Break Escape activates.
The player escapes the combo and gains brief invulnerability.

The longer you hold LMB + RMB, the higher the Break level becomes, but the more resource you spend.

Break Levels

Maximum Break Resource: 300

Level 1 Break

Cost: 100 Resource

Requires holding LMB + RMB until the first stage is reached.
Quick escape option.
Stops combos early while preserving resource.

Example:

Resource: 300
Enemy lands a hit → Hold LMB + RMB
Reach Level 1 → Break Escape activates

Cost: -100
Remaining: 200
Level 2 Break

Cost: 200 Resource

Requires holding LMB + RMB longer until the second stage is reached.
Used against stronger or longer combos.
Provides a safer escape but uses more resource.

Example:

Resource: 300
Enemy lands a hit → Hold LMB + RMB
Reach Level 2 → Break Escape activates

Cost: -200
Remaining: 100
Level 3 Break

Cost: 300 Resource

Requires holding LMB + RMB for the longest duration.
Emergency escape option.
Completely drains your resource but gives the strongest escape.

Example:

Resource: 300
Enemy lands a hit → Hold LMB + RMB
Reach Level 3 → Break Escape activates

Cost: -300
Remaining: 0
Combat Strategy

Break Escape creates a mind game between attackers and defenders.

Attackers:

Must successfully land hits to pressure the opponent.
Can try to bait the defender into wasting Break Resource.
Become more dangerous once the opponent has no resource left.

Defenders:

Must decide when to hold LMB + RMB and escape.
Using Break Escape too early wastes resources.
Waiting too long risks taking heavy damage.

The key decision is:

"Is avoiding this combo worth spending my Break Resource?"
